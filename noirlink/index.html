Before you begin

A few things
worth knowing.

Get familiar with the controls and the information shown in Noir Link.

What does Noir Link bring together?

VPN connection controls, available server entries, speed testing, weekly usage, recent sessions, a browser with bookmarks, and language preferences.

What do the speed-test numbers mean?

The test reports download speed, upload speed and ping for current network conditions. Readings can vary and do not guarantee future performance.

Which routes can I choose?

Open the server screen in the app to see its available entries and latency labels. Availability may change; this page does not promise a specific location or number of servers.

Can I change the app language?

Yes. The language menu includes a system-default option and a list of available languages.
